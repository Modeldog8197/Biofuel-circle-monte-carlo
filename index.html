05 / Research notes

A forecast should be inspectable.

01

Keep time in order

Models train only on months before each forecast origin. Six candidates compete on the development period; the final eight months provide a separate, rolling evaluation.

02

Preserve joint errors

Each path resamples a complete four-month error vector. Errors scale with the latest level and retain dependence across horizons. Counts are rounded and all outcomes remain nonnegative.

03

Measure the limitations

Overlapping forecasts are not independent observations. The sample is small and several interval coverages miss their nominal level. More paths reduce simulation noise; they do not repair a weak model.

Totals sum each complete simulated path before taking percentiles. They never add monthly interval endpoints. Cross-metric totals are not computed because dependence between targets is not modelled.
