Method notes

Six candidates are compared on chronological development errors. Later evaluation uses five expanding four-month origins: the 20 target–horizon outcomes overlap and are not independent samples. Manual model comparisons are exploratory.

Each simulation samples a complete historical four-month error vector. The paths preserve cross-horizon dependence within a target, remain nonnegative and round counts. More paths reduce numerical sampling noise, not model error. Bands under-cover several evaluation outcomes; probabilities are not calibrated guarantees.

Four-month totals sum each path before taking percentiles. No cross-target aggregate is computed. Central paths are a clearly labelled subset for visibility; distributions, thresholds, tables and exports use all paths.

Relationships and lag heatmaps use observed pairs, exclude gaps, and expose levels versus differences. Scenario coefficients are fitted only through the development cutoff, with standardized predictor changes and ridge penalty 10. Future driver changes and their persistence are user assumptions; scenario accuracy has not been established.
